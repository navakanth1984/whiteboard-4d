BLEUUBOARD 4D

Next-Gen Spatial Collaboration Studio  ·  STUDIO MANUAL

A unified 3D spatial studio fusing Gaussian Splats, AI Vision, Real-Time Physics, WebRTC Multiplayer, and 4D Temporal Time-Travel.

  • 3 Camera POVs — Switch between FPS (1st person), TPS (3rd person character), and Orbit (studio view).
  • Gamer Flight Locomotion — Fly freely with WASD, ascend with Space, descend with C, or use the 3D Flight D-Pad.
  • Multi-Touch & Mobile Tiering — 1-finger rotate, 2-finger pinch/twist, with auto DPR scaling for 60 FPS mobile performance.
  • Isometric Holomap Radar — 1-click teleportation to any spatial sector across 100m world coordinates.
✨

3D Creation, Splats & Live Code

  • 3D Ink & Extruded Text — Paint glowing 3D tubes in mid-air or place extruded solid typography anywhere.
  • 3D Gaussian Splatting (.SPZ) — Place and manipulate photorealistic 3D point cloud scans with real-time 3D gizmos.
  • Interactive Live Code Sandbox — Place glass JavaScript terminals in space and execute live code in real time.
  • 3D Multi-Page Flip Decks — Place interactive presentation books with realistic 3D page curl rotations.
  • Tech & AI Nodes — Place glowing architecture nodes and connect them with animated particle signal flows.
🤖

AI Copilot, Vision & 3D OCR

  • WebCam & Mobile Hand Tracking — Enable HAND TRACK to pinch-to-draw and pinch-to-grab objects using Google MediaPipe.
  • In-World AI Spatial Copilot — Toggle COPILOT for an autonomous 3D companion drone with voice and layout intelligence.
  • 3D Handwriting-to-Typography OCR — Draw rough sketches or text, then click 3D OCR to convert them to clean 3D typography.
  • VisionOS 3D Orbital Action Menu — Select any object to open a floating 6-action radial menu (Duplicate, Toss, Connect, Tint, OCR, Dissolve).
⚡

Physics, Avatars & Holodecks

  • Rapier.js 3D Physics Simulation — WASM rigid body physics with dynamic bounding colliders, zero-g drift, and impulse toss.
  • AccuRig & SkinnedMesh Rigging — Industry-standard bone hierarchies with walking, idle, and jump kinematics.
  • VRM Anime & Cyberpunk Avatars — Load humanoid `.vrm` characters with SpringBone hair/cloth physics and procedural blinking.
  • 360° Holodeck Atmospheres — Switch between Cyber Void, Vaporwave Sunset, Architect Studio, and Deep Space Nebula.
🌐

WebXR, Multiplayer & Audio

  • WebXR Spatial VR/AR Mode — Enter full immersion on Apple Vision Pro or Meta Quest 3 with 6DoF motion controllers and laser rays.
  • Real-Time WebRTC Multiplayer — P2P mesh network with 20Hz avatar transform broadcasting and wireframe peer visualization.
  • 3D Positional Audio Cones — Web Audio `Panner3D` with inverse distance attenuation and 180° directional audio cones.
  • Procedural Cyber Micro-Audio & Haptics — In-memory synthesized audio feedback (hover pops, clicks, whooshes, chimes) + mobile vibration.
⏱️

4D Time-Travel & 1-Click GLB Export

  • 4D Temporal Time-Travel Scrubber — Rewind, fast-forward, and play back spatial construction history at 1x/2x/4x speed.
  • 1-Click 3D Scene GLB / GLTF Export — Export your entire multi-object 3D scene directly into Blender, Unreal Engine, or Unity.
  • Selective Neon Bloom Post-Processing — Lush Three.js r180 bloom effects for lasers, cards, and glowing nodes.
  • Persistent Browser Sessions — Save complete sessions to localStorage or download standalone JSON files.
